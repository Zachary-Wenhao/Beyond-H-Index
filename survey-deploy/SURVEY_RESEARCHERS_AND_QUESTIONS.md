## Notes on Researcher Content

- The **full narrative profiles** (position, field & expertise, career overview, publication record, citation impact, and current activity) for each researcher are defined in `index.html` under the corresponding `<!-- Comparison X: ... -->` blocks.
- When the survey is in **anonymous mode**, participants see only “Researcher A” and “Researcher B” along with either full metrics or minimal info, depending on the randomized condition.
- When in **named mode**, the `researcherNames` map at the bottom of `index.html` controls which real names are shown for each comparison.
